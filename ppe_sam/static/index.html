      Advanced users only: The settings within this page should NOT be changed without explicit PM approval. The settings and assumptions below are PPE default values, which provide a conservative estimate of Isc and Voc. Most rows and assumptions are locked, but a few can be changed if your site needs a get-out-of-jail free card, or to get over the hump on wire sizing.
